About me

I'm a Java developer with over seven years of experience building enterprise applications with Java EE, SOAP and REST services, and relational databases. I've worked on both large monolithic systems and on their gradual transition towards microservices.

Before focusing fully on development I worked in software testing and technical support. That background shapes how I write code today: I care about edge cases, clear error handling and systems that stay reliable in production, not just in the demo.

Since 2021 I've been part of a backend team working on a large e-commerce and incentive platform used by corporate customers across Europe.

Outside of work I run marathons and read history. Both have taught me the same lesson: steady, consistent effort beats sprints.
